About the University Clinic

The CTU Main Medical & Dental Clinic at CTU – Main Campus looks after the health and wellbeing of students, faculty, and personnel — from routine check-ups and dental care to first aid, health counseling, and campus-wide wellness programs. CampusCare is the clinic's online companion: it brings appointment booking, health records, certificate requests, and clinic announcements to your phone, so you spend less time waiting in line and more time in class.

Referral & Emergency Advisory
For persistent symptoms or conditions that need further evaluation, the clinic will refer you to the nearest partner medical facility and notify your emergency contact. In case of an emergency on campus, proceed directly to the clinic or alert any university personnel immediately. — CTU Main Medical & Dental Clinic, CTU Main Campus
