Clinic Services

What the clinic offers — and how CampusCare makes each service easier to access.

Medical Consultation & Check-ups

Free consultations, vital signs monitoring, and health assessments with campus physicians. Book a slot online and reduce front-desk waiting.

Dental Services

Dental check-ups, restorative care, surgery, oral prophylaxis, oral examination, referral, and oral-health advice. Availability depends on the clinic schedule and service.

First Aid & Emergency Care

Immediate response to minor injuries, wounds, and sudden illness during clinic hours. Emergency walk-ins do not require an appointment.

Medical Certificates & Clearances

Request certificates for OJT, sports, enrollment, and excused absence, then follow the preparation and Doctor-signature workflow online.

Health Records

Consultations, treatments, prescriptions, vitals, and follow-up information are organized in one role-protected clinical record.

Health Education & Wellness

Access clinic advisories and trustworthy educational resources covering stress, nutrition, first aid, vaccination, sleep, and physical activity.

About the Clinic

Campus care, connected in one place

CampusCare supports the CTU Main Medical & Dental Clinic by bringing its most common student and personnel workflows into one secure system.

CTU Main Medical & Dental Clinic

Located on the Ground Floor of the Education Building at CTU Main Campus, the clinic supports students, faculty, and personnel through routine consultations, dental care, first aid, health guidance, medical certificates, fitness assessments, and wellness initiatives.

CampusCare serves as the clinic's digital companion so patients can complete common tasks before and after an in-person visit while clinic personnel keep records, workflows, and communication organized.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions before visiting or using CampusCare.

Do I always need an appointment?

Routine consultations and limited dental slots are easier to coordinate through CampusCare. Emergency concerns and urgent walk-ins during clinic hours do not require an appointment.

What should I bring to the clinic?

Bring your COR and school/employee ID. Some services, such as fitness assessments or certificates, may require additional forms or supporting documents.

Can the clinic refer me to another facility?

Yes. Concerns that require further examination, diagnostics, or care outside the campus clinic may be referred to an appropriate medical facility.

Where can I report a concern about CampusCare?

After signing in, use Report an Issue. Privacy or personal-data requests are available from Account Settings.
